## Business problem
Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market and has two weeks of handwritten sales and stock records. She wants to know how her business is doing, whether it is growing, what she should restock, and where she should focus her effort. This project analyses her data using only core Python.

## How to run
[Open in Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/github/Daniel-Kinara/plp-da-a1-gikomba-stall-analysis/blob/main/stall_analysis.ipynb)

## Top three findings
1. Sales grew **10.66%** week on week (KES 22,050 → KES 24,400).
2. Weekend days average **KES 4,900** versus **KES 2,685** on weekdays, which is **82.5%** higher.
3. **Tomatoes** (18 kg vs 25 kg) and **Sukuma** (9 kg vs 15 kg) are below reorder level; Avocado has the best margin at **42.86%**.
